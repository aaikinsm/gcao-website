Overview

Four homepage design directions for the GCAO website redesign

Each preview uses the same GCAO content so you compare design direction, not copy. All four are mobile-responsive and use the official GCAO circular logo and brand colors (red, gold, green).

How to Review

  1. Open each live preview URL on desktop and phone
  2. Check: Does it feel modern? Easy to navigate? Trustworthy?
  3. Consider your audience: donors, families, youth, seniors
  4. Pick Option A, B, C, or D on the final page

Option A

Premium, story-driven, editorial

Design Characteristics

  • Full-bleed cinematic hero with large editorial headline
  • Mission statement as a prominent blockquote
  • Story-driven news cards with category tags on dark background
  • Minimal navigation, single Donate CTA
  • Navy + gold palette — premium, warm, institutional

Best For

Donor trust, storytelling, board-facing polish, organizations emphasizing legacy and impact narratives.

Option B

Bold, energetic, program-first

Design Characteristics

  • Question-led hero: "How do we strengthen Ghanaian-Canadian communities?"
  • Bold uppercase headings, green header bar
  • Three pillar cards with colored top borders
  • Impact stats row, featured program with "Register Now"
  • Full Ghana flag palette — energetic, action-oriented

Best For

Volunteers, program participants, youth and families, organizations prioritizing program enrollment and community engagement.

Option C

Modern balance of layout clarity and community energy

Design Characteristics

  • Split hero: mission headline + community photo side by side
  • Clean white header, green + gold accents
  • Card-based program grid with hover effects
  • Featured event banner, 2-column news layout
  • Resource Hub callout with contact details

Best For

Broad audience — seniors, youth, donors, and first-time visitors. Balances polish with approachability.

Option D

Cultural identity-forward · Ghanaian-Canadian belonging

Design Characteristics

  • Hero: "Where Ghanaian roots meet Canadian community" with kente-inspired accent bands
  • Flag-color stripe beneath navigation (red, gold, green)
  • Pillar cards with colored left borders in flag palette
  • Intergenerational photo grid, serif display headings
  • Full Ghana palette on warm cream — identity and culture at the forefront

Best For

Community members who want the site to reflect Ghanaian-Canadian identity prominently — cultural events, diaspora initiatives, and intergenerational belonging.
